Deactivate Account?

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Deactivating your account will immediately hide your restaurant from the platform and cancel all pending orders.

Consequences of Deactivation:

  • cancel Lose access to entire order history and analytics.
  • visibility_off Immediate removal of menu visibility from the app.
  • payments Pending payouts will be processed, but new earnings will stop.
