How do I withdraw my earnings?

Withdrawing your hard-earned funds is designed to be seamless. You have two primary options to access your earnings, depending on how quickly you need the funds.

Option 1: Instant Cashout

Need your money right now? The Instant Cashout feature transfers your current available balance to your linked debit card within minutes.

  • Go to the Earnings tab in the Bottom Navigation.
  • Tap the blue Cash Out button.
  • Select Instant Transfer.
  • Confirm the amount. A small processing fee of $0.50 applies per instant transfer.
  • Funds will appear in your linked bank account typically within 15-30 minutes.

Option 2: Weekly Automatic Direct Deposit

If you prefer a hands-off approach, we automatically initiate a direct deposit every week.

  • The payout period ends every Monday at 4:00 AM local time.
  • Any funds remaining in your balance are automatically transferred to your primary linked bank account.
  • This transfer is completely free.
  • Funds typically clear and become available in your account by Wednesday evening or Thursday morning, depending on your bank's processing times.
info

Important Note

Ensure your bank account or debit card details are up-to-date in your Account Settings to prevent any delays in processing your withdrawals.
