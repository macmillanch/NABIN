Kitchen Troubleshooting Guide

Step-by-step

Thermal paper is chemically coated on only one side. If slips eject without black text:

  1. Flip open the top latch using the right green release toggle.
  2. Ensure paper feeds from the bottom of the roll upward towards the cutter edge.
  3. Perform a finger scratch test: scratching the heat-sensitive side leaves a faint black mark.

When the internal rotary cutter stalls during a heavy grease or ticket pile-up:

info

Power OFF device. Pop open the small front panel cover and turn the white knurled knob until the blade returns fully to home position before forcing the latch open.
